Stimulus Tracker

Source, produce, review, and clear every score and audio stimulus from first idea to item bank.

About this tool

Built as a work sample for the administrative side of an AP Music Theory assessment role: the stimulus pipeline that runs from sourcing a score or recording, through Sibelius engraving, Illustrator annotation, audio editing, and alt text, into internal and committee review, permissions clearance, and placement in the item bank.

  • Production checklist: the five boxes on each row track Score (Sibelius), SVG annotation (Illustrator), Audio (Audition / Media Encoder), Alt text, and Item bank placement.
  • At-risk flags: a stimulus is flagged when its due date is within 14 days and it is not ready, or when a permissions request has been waiting more than 30 days.
  • Permissions log: every third-party stimulus shows request date, days waiting, rights holder, and expiration, so nothing goes into an item without clearance.
  • Portable: data saves in your browser; export to CSV for the permissions team or a project plan.
